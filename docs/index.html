Download

WorldSimulator runs fully on every device. Minimum requirements: 8 GB RAM, an Apple M1 or equivalent Intel/AMD processor, and ~10 GB of storage. Live news, the three local AI models, scenario branching, and the 2.5D map all run on that minimum. Everything stays on your machine.

What it is

WorldSimulator is a local, offline desktop application that simulates alternate history. You type a divergence (“what if the Nazis won World War II?”, “what if Rome never fell?”) and the engine reasons about the consequences, branches the timeline into several plausible outcomes, and renders the changing world on an interactive 2.5‑dimensional map.

Everything runs on your machine. No cloud, no API keys, no telemetry. The intelligence comes from three small language models that are fine‑tuned for this exact job and bundled with the app.

What you can do

  • Diverge. Type a prompt → a scenario is created at a divergence date and simulated forward to 2100 across several parallel branches.
  • Explore the map. Territories are 2.5D blocks, colored by owner and raised by population. Pan, zoom, tilt, and click.
  • Scroll time. The timeline spans two million years with era markers.
  • Compare branches. Each branch is diffed against the canonical world.
  • Predict the future. Live RSS news is fetched, scored for trust, and can be seeded into a near‑future scenario.
  • Stay offline. No network call is required at runtime.
